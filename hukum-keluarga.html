Hukum Keluarga, Pernikahan & Waris

Pasal 127 hingga 193 mencakup sepertiga dari seluruh prasasti monolit: keabsahan akad nikah, perceraian, perlindungan istri sakit kronis, status kemerdekaan anak budak, hak janda dan anak yatim, serta hukum adopsi.

Logika Sosial: Hak Perdata Perempuan & Keturunan

Masyarakat Babilonia bersifat patriarki, namun hukum Hammurabi memberikan perlindungan perdata bagi perempuan yang jauh lebih maju dibanding peradaban sezamannya. Pernikahan tanpa kontrak tertulis dianggap tidak sah (Pasal 128). Mahar (sheriktu) dari ayah istri tetap menjadi milik pribadi sang istri dan diwariskan ke anak-anaknya, bukan milik keluarga suami (Pasal 162).

Pasal 148 melarang suami menelantarkan atau menceraikan istri yang sakit parah. Jika budak menikah dengan wanita merdeka, anak-anak mereka lahir sebagai orang merdeka (Pasal 175). Janda dilindungi dari pengusiran oleh anak-anak kandungnya (Pasal 172) dan suami baru dilarang memperjualbelikan warisan anak tiri (Pasal 177).

Pasal 127

"If a man point the finger at a votary or the wife of another and cannot justify it, they shall drag that man before the judges and they shall brand his forehead."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Memfitnah wanita suci atau istri orang tanpa bukti: pelaku diseret ke pengadilan dan dahinya dicap tanda cela/aib permanen.

128

Pasal 128 ★

Keabsahan Akad Nikah
"If a man take a wife and do not arrange with her the (proper) contracts, that woman is not a (legal) wife."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seorang pria mengambil seorang wanita untuk dijadikan istri tetapi tidak membuat akta kontrak pernikahan tertulis bersamanya, maka wanita tersebut secara hukum BUKANLAH ISTRI SAH.

Konteks: Syarat mutlak akta tertulis demi kepastian hukum waris dan status anak.

Pasal 129

Perzinaan
"If the wife of a man be taken in lying with another man, they shall bind them and throw them into the water. If the husband of the woman would save his wife, or if the king would save his male servant (he may)."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Istri yang tertangkap basah berzina diikat bersama pria selingkuhannya dan ditenggelamkan ke air; kecuali suami memaafkan istrinya, dan raja mengampuni hambanya.

Pasal 130

Pasal 131

"If a man accuse his wife and she has not been taken in lying with another man, she shall take an oath in the name of the god and she shall return to her house."

Pasal 132

"If the finger have been pointed at the wife of a man because of another man... for her husband's sake she shall throw herself into the river."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika desas-desus publik menuduh istri berzina, demi memulihkan nama suaminya ia menjalani ujian sungai suci (River Ordeal).

Pasal 133 & 133A

"If a man be captured and there be maintenance in his house and his wife go out of her house, she shall protect her body (?)... (133A) If that woman do not protect her body and enter into another house, they shall call that woman to account and they shall throw her into the water."

Jika suami ditawan musuh dan di rumah ada nafkah cukup, istri wajib menjaga kehormatan; jika ia kawin lagi padahal nafkah ada, ia dihukum.

Pasal 134

"If a man be captured and there be no maintenance in his house, and his wife enter into another house, that woman has no blame."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika suami tertawan dan TIDAK ADA NAFKAH di rumah, istri boleh menikah dengan pria lain dan TIDAK BERSALAH sama sekali.

Pasal 135

"If a man be captured and there be no maintenance... and his wife openly enter into another house and bear children; if later her husband return... that woman shall return to her husband (and) the children shall go to their father."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika suami pertama kembali setelah lama tertawan, wanita itu kembali ke suami pertamanya, sedangkan anak-anak dari pernikahan kedua tetap bersama ayah kandung mereka.

Pasal 136

"If a man desert his city and flee, and afterwards his wife enter into another house; if that man return... the wife of the fugitive shall not return to her husband, because he hated his city and fled."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Suami yang kabur meninggalkan kotanya kehilangan hak atas istrinya; istri tidak boleh dipaksa kembali kepadanya karena ia telah mengkhianati tanah airnya.

137

Pasal 137

"If a man set his face to put away a concubine who has borne him children, or a wife who has presented him with children, he shall return to that woman her dowry and shall give to her the income of field, garden, and goods, and she shall bring up her children..."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Menceraikan istri atau selir yang telah melahirkan anak: suami wajib mengembalikan mahar dan memberi hak atas hasil kebun/harta untuk membesarkan anak-anaknya.

138

Pasal 138–140

"If a man would put away his wife who has not borne him children, he shall give her money to the amount of her marriage settlement, and he shall make good to her the dowry... (139) If there were no marriage settlement, he shall give to her 1 mana of silver for a divorce. (140) If he be a freeman, he shall give her one-third mana of silver."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Kompensasi perceraian istri tanpa anak: kembalikan mahar; jika tidak ada uang mahar, denda talak 1 mana perak untuk bangsawan (awilum) dan 1/3 mana perak untuk rakyat jelata (mushkenum).

141

Pasal 141–143

"If the wife of a man... set her face to go out and play the part of a fool, neglect her house, belittle her husband... (142) If a woman hate her husband, and say: 'Thou shalt not have me'... and if she have been a careful mistress... she shall receive her dowry and shall go to her father's house. (143) If she have not been a careful mistress... they shall throw that woman into the water."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Penyelidikan latar belakang istri yang menuntut cerai: jika istri terbukti rajin dan suaminya yang gemar menghina, istri berhak mengambil maharnya dan pulang ke rumah ayahnya; jika istri yang lalai dan berfoya-foya, ia dihukum.

144

Pasal 144–147

"If a man take a wife and that wife give a maid-servant to her husband and she bear children; if that man set his face to take a concubine, they shall not countenance him... (146) If that maid-servant bear children and afterwards would take rank with her mistress... her mistress may not sell her for money, but she may reduce her to bondage..."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Aturan poligini dan selir budak (mirip kisah Sarah dan Hagar dalam Kitab Kejadian 16): budak yang telah melahirkan anak bagi suami dilarang dijual, tetapi statusnya tetap sebagai pelayan jika bersikap sombong pada nyonyanya.

148

Pasal 148 ★

Perlindungan Istri Sakit
"If a man take a wife and she become afflicted with disease, and if he set his face to take another, he may. His wife, who is afflicted with disease, he shall not put away. She shall remain in the house which he has built and he shall maintain her as long as she lives."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seorang pria menikahi wanita dan wanita itu mengidap penyakit parah/kronis, lalu pria itu berniat menikah lagi dengan wanita lain, ia diizinkan. TETAPI ISTRI YANG SAKIT ITU SAMA SEKALI TIDAK BOLEH DICERAIKAN ATAU DIUSIR. Wanita itu harus tetap tinggal di rumah yang dibangun suaminya, dan sang suami WAJIB MEMBERINYA NAFKAH SEUMUR HIDUPNYA.

Konteks: Klausul jaminan kesehatan dan perlindungan wanita paling luhur dalam prasasti kuno.

149

Pasal 149–153

"If that woman do not elect to remain in her husband's house, he shall make good to her the dowry... (150) If a man give to his wife field, garden, house... after the death of her husband, her children cannot make claim against her... (153) If a woman bring about the death of her husband for the sake of another man, they shall impale her."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Pasal 149: Istri sakit boleh memilih pulang ke rumah ayahnya dengan membawa penuh maharnya. Pasal 150: Hibah resmi suami kepada istri bersegel tidak boleh digugat anak-anaknya. Pasal 153: Istri yang berkomplot membunuh suami demi pria idaman lain dihukum mati dengan cara disula.

154

Pasal 154–158

Larangan Inses
"(154) If a man have known his daughter, they shall expel that man from the city. (155) If a man have betrothed a bride to his son and his son have known her, and if he afterward lie in her bosom... they shall bind that man and throw him into the water. (157) If a man lie in the bosom of his mother after the death of his father, they shall burn both of them."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Inses dengan anak kandung (diusir dari kota); menyetubuhi menantu yang sudah digauli anak kandungnya (ditenggelamkan ke air); bersetubuh dengan ibu kandung setelah ayah wafat (keduanya dibakar hidup-hidup).

159

Pasal 159–164

"(159) If a man... look with longing upon another woman and say to his father-in-law, 'I will not take thy daughter'; the father shall take to himself whatever was brought... (160) If the father of the daughter say, 'I will not give thee my daughter'; he shall double the amount... and return it."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Pembatalan perkawinan: jika calon suami yang membatalkan, uang lamaran hangus; jika calon mertua yang membatalkan, mertua wajib mengembalikan dua kali lipat uang lamaran tersebut.

165

Pasal 165–169

"(165) If a man present field, garden, or house to his favourite son, and write for him a sealed deed... he shall take the present... and over and above they shall divide the goods of the father's house equally. (168) If a man set his face to disinherit his son... the judges shall inquire... and condone his first offence."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Hadiah khusus berakta bagi anak kesayangan; perlindungan anak dari pencoretan waris sepihak (hakim wajib memaafkan pelanggaran pertama sang anak, Pasal 169).

170

Pasal 170 & 171 ★

Anak dari Budak Wanita
"(170) If a man's wife bear him children and his maid-servant bear him children, and the father during his life time say... 'My children'... the children of the wife and the children of the maid-servant shall divide the goods of the father's house equally... (171) But if the father during his lifetime have not said... 'My children'... after the father dies, the children of the maid-servant shall not share in the goods... The maid-servant and her children shall be given their freedom. The children of the wife may not lay claim to the children of the maid-servant for service."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika sang ayah semasa hidupnya mengakui anak dari budak perempuannya dengan menyebut 'Anak-anakku', mereka mewarisi harta sama rata dengan anak istri sah. Jika ayah tidak mengakuinya, mereka tidak mendapat warisan, TETAPI BUDAK PEREMPUAN ITU BESERTA ANAK-ANAKNYA WAJIB DIBERI KEMERDEKAAN MUTLAK! Anak-anak istri sah dilarang memperbudak mereka.

172

Pasal 172–174

"(172) If her husband have not given her a gift, they shall make good her dowry and she shall receive from the goods of her husband's house a portion corresponding to that of her son. If her children scheme to drive her out... the judges shall inquire... and she shall not go from her husband's house."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Perlindungan janda: anak-anak dilarang mengusir ibu kandung mereka dari rumah almarhum ayahnya.

175

Pasal 175 & 176 ★

Pernikahan Lintas Kasta
"If either a slave of the palace or a slave of a freeman take the daughter of a man (gentleman) and she bear children, the owner of the slave may not lay claim to the children of the daughter of the man for service... (176) the owner of the slave shall receive one-half and the daughter of the man shall receive one-half for her children."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seorang budak istana atau budak orang merdeka menikahi putri seorang bangsawan/warga merdeka dan melahirkan anak, MAJIKAN BUDAK DILARANG MENGKLAIM ANAK-ANAK MEREKA SEBAGAI BUDAK! Harta yang dikumpulkan bersama selama perkawinan dibagi dua: separuh untuk majikan budak dan separuh untuk sang istri dan anak-anak merdekanya.

177

Pasal 177

"If a widow, whose children are minors, set her face to enter another house, she cannot do so without the consent of the judges... They may not sell the household goods. He who purchases household goods belonging to the sons of a widow shall forfeit his money."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Perwalian anak yatim: janda yang menikah lagi wajib didaftar hartanya oleh hakim; barang milik anak yatim dilarang dijual oleh ayah tiri.

178

Pasal 178–184

"If a votary or a devotee to whom her father has given a dowry... after her father dies her brothers shall give her grain, oil, and wool... (182) A votary of Marduk of Babylon, after her (death), may give to whomsoever she may please."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Hak properti dan nafkah bagi pendeta wanita kuil dan perawan suci Marduk.

185

Pasal 185–189

"(185) If a man take in his name a young child as a son, and rear him, one may not bring claim for that adopted son. (188) If an artisan take a son for adoption and teach him his handicraft, one may not bring claim for him. (189) If he do not teach him his handicraft, that adopted son may return to his father's house."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Hukum pengadopsian anak dan magang pengrajin: jika pengrajin mengadopsi anak dan mengajarkan keahlian pertukangan, adopsi bersifat permanen; jika lalai mengajarkan keahlian, anak boleh kembali ke orang tua kandungnya.

190

Pasal 190–193

"(191) If a man who has taken a young child as a son and reared him, establish his own house and acquire children, and set his face to cut off the adopted son... he shall give to him of his goods one-third the portion of a son and he shall go... (192) If the son of a palace guard say to his foster father... 'My father thou art not'... they shall cut out his tongue."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Pasal 191: Orang tua angkat yang memiliki anak kandung dilarang mengusir anak adopsi dengan tangan hampa (wajib memberi 1/3 bagian warisan). Pasal 192–193: Anak adopsi penjaga istana yang durhaka menyangkal orang tua angkatnya dipotong lidahnya atau dicungkil matanya.
