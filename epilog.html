Kolom 41 Monolit Diorit

Analisis Editorial: Mengapa Epilog Memuat Kutukan yang Sangat Panjang?

Hampir separuh dari teks epilog bukanlah pidato keadilan, melainkan kutukan berantai terhadap raja masa depan yang berani merusak atau menghapus hukum Hammurabi. Bagi sejarawan modern, ini memperlihatkan kecemasan akut seorang pembaharu politik kuno: Hammurabi sadar betul bahwa di dunia Mesopotamia yang bergolak, monumen mudah sekali dihancurkan atau dinamai ulang oleh penguasa penakluk berikutnya.

Ironisnya, ketakutan Hammurabi terbukti nyata sekitar 600 tahun kemudian: Raja Elam Shutruk-Nakhkhunte benar-benar menjarah monolit ini ke Susa dan menghapus 5 kolom teksnya (klausul 66–99). Namun kutukan para dewa terbukti 'menjaga' sisa teksnya tetap utuh dan terbaca hingga ditemukan pada tahun 1901.

TEKS TERJEMAHAN EPILOG (R.F. HARPER) Kolom 40–44 Monolit

The righteous laws, which Hammurabi, the wise king, established and (by which) he gave the land stable support and pure government. Hammurabi, the perfect king, am I. I was not careless, nor was I neglectful of the Black Head people, whose rule Enlil presented and Marduk delivered to me. I provided them with a peaceful country... in my wisdom I restrained them; that the strong might not oppress the weak, and that they should give justice to the orphan and the widow...

(Col. 41.) Let any oppressed man, who has a cause, come before my image as king of righteousness! Let him read the inscriptions on my monument! Let him give heed to my weighty words! And may my monument enlighten him as to his cause, and may he understand his case! May he set his heart at ease! (and he will exclaim): "Hammurabi indeed is a ruler, who is like a real father to his people..."

In the days that are yet to come, for all future time, may the king who is in the land observe the words of righteousness which I have written upon my monument! May he not alter the judgments of the land which I have pronounced, or the decisions of the country which I have rendered! May he not efface my statues!

If that man do not pay attention to my words which I have written upon my monument; if he forget my curse and do not fear the curse of god; if he abolish the judgments which I have formulated, overrule my words, alter my statues, efface my name written thereon and write his own name... as for that man, be he king or lord, or priest-king or commoner, whoever he may be:

May the great god, the father of the gods, who has ordained my reign, take from him the glory of his sovereignty, may he break his sceptre and curse his fate!

May Enlil, the lord, who determines destinies, whose command cannot be altered... drive him out from his dwelling through a revolt which his hand cannot control and a curse destructive to him! May he determine as his fate a reign of sighs, days few in number, years of famine, darkness without light, death staring him in the face!

May Belit, the august mother, whose command is potent in E-kur... put into the mouth of Enlil, the king, the ruin of his land, the destruction of his people, and the pouring out of his life like water!

May Ea, the great prince, whose decrees take precedence... deprive him of knowledge and wisdom! May he bring him to oblivion, and dam up his rivers at their sources! May he not permit corn, which is the life of the people, to grow in his land!

May Shamash, the great judge of heaven and earth... overthrow his dominion; may he not grant him his rights! May he make him to err in his path, may he destroy the foundation of his troops! Below, within the earth, may he deprive his spirit of water!

May Sin, the lord of heaven, my divine creator... take away from him the crown and throne of sovereignty! May he lay upon him heavy guilt and great sin... May he determine as his fate a life like unto death!

May Adad, the lord of abundance... deprive him of the rain from heaven and the water-floods from the springs! May he bring his land to destruction through want and hunger! May he turn his land into a heap left by a whirlwind!

May Za-ma-ma, the great warrior, and Ishtar, goddess of battle and conflict... shatter his weapons on the field of battle! May she strike down his warriors, may their blood water the earth! May she cast the bodies of his warriors upon the field in heaps! May she not grant his warriors burial! May she deliver him into the hands of his enemies!

May Nergal, the mighty among the gods, the warrior without an equal... burn his people like a raging fire of swamp-reed! With his powerful weapon, may he cut him off and break his members like an earthen image!

May Nin-tu, the exalted mistress of the lands, the mother who bore me, deny him a son! May she not let him hold a name among his people, nor beget an heir!

May Nin-kar-ra-sa, the daughter of Anu... cause to come upon his members until it overcomes his life a grievous malady, an evil disease, a dangerous sore, which cannot be cured, which the physician cannot diagnose, which he cannot allay with bandages, and which, like the bite of death, cannot be removed! May he lament the loss of his vigour! May Enlil curse him with a powerful curse, and may it come upon him speedily!

Terjemahan Lengkap Bahasa Indonesia

"Undang-undang keadilan yang ditetapkan oleh Hammurabi, sang raja bijaksana, yang dengannya ia memberi negeri ini fondasi yang kokoh dan pemerintahan yang bersih. Akulah Hammurabi, sang raja yang sempurna. Aku tidak lalai, dan tidak pula mengabaikan bangsa kepala-hitam yang pemerintahannya dipercayakan oleh Enlil dan diserahkan Marduk kepadaku... Agar yang kuat tidak menindas yang lemah, dan agar keadilan diberikan kepada anak yatim dan para janda di Babilonia..."

"Biarlah setiap orang tertindas yang memiliki perkara datang di hadapan patungku... membaca prasasti pada monumen ini... dan berseru: 'Hammurabi sungguh seorang penguasa yang laksana ayah sejati bagi rakyatnya!'"

"Bagi siapa pun di masa depan yang menghapus hukumku ini, mengganti namaku dengan namanya, kiranya dewa-dewa mengutuknya: Enlil mengirim kelaparan dan maut; Ea mengeringkan mata air sungai; Shamash menolak arwahnya di alam baka; Adad mengirim badai kemarau; Ishtar membiarkan mayat pasukannya membusuk tanpa dikubur; Nergal membakarnya laksana api alang-alang; Nintu meniadakan keturunannya; dan Ninkarrasa menimpakan penyakit ganas yang tak sanggup diobati tabib mana pun!"

Daftar Kutukan Sakral 10 Dewa Babilonia

Dewa Kedudukan Ilahi Bentuk Kutukan Terhadap Perusak Hukum
Enlil Penguasa Takdir Bumi Pemberontakan tak terkendali, tahun-tahun kelaparan panjang, dan penghapusan namanya dari sejarah.
Belit Ibu Agung Istana E-kur Memutarbalikkan doanya di depan Enlil dan menumpahkan hidupnya laksana air di tanah.
Ea Dewa Air Tawar & Hikmat Melenyapkan kebijaksanaannya, membendung sumber mata air sungai, dan memusnahkan tanaman gandum.
Shamash Hakim Agung Matahari Menghancurkan tentaranya dan menolak memberi seteguk air pun bagi arwahnya di alam baka (bawah tanah).
Sin Dewa Bulan & Mahkota Mencabut mahkota tahta, menimpakan dosa berat tak berampun, dan menjadikan hidupnya 'seperti maut'.
Adad Dewa Badai & Hujan Menahan hujan langit, menimbulkan kekeringan parah, lalu menyapu tanahnya dengan badai angin puyuh.
Ishtar & Zamama Dewa-Dewi Peperangan Mematahkan senjatanya, menumpuk mayat pasukannya tanpa dikubur, dan menyerahkannya tertawan ke musuh.
Nergal Dewa Maut & Dunia Bawah Membakar rakyatnya bagaikan api mengamuk di rawa-rawa dan meremukkan tubuhnya seperti tembikar lempung.
Nintu Dewi Rahim & Kelahiran Membuatnya mandul tanpa keturunan laki-laki sehingga dinasti dan garis keturunannya musnah selamanya.
Ninkarrasa Dewi Kedokteran (Putri Anu) Menimpakan borok ganas beracun yang tidak dapat didiagnosis tabib dan tidak bisa dibebat perban sampai ajal menjemput.
