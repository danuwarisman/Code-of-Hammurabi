Kategori 8 dari 14

Perdagangan, Utang & Kedai Tuak

Pasal 100 hingga 126 mengatur hubungan kemitraan pemodal dan agen, pengawasan ketat kedai bir, perlindungan debitur dari jeratan seumur hidup (Pasal 117), dan hukum penitipan barang (bailment).

Logika Sosial: Kapitalisme Kuno & Etika Transaksi

Babilonia adalah imperium niaga terdepan. Hukum mewajibkan tanda terima bersegel (Pasal 105: uang tanpa kuitansi tidak sah). Pasal 108–110 menyoroti kedai bir yang hampir selalu dijalankan oleh wanita pengusaha (sabītu); manipulasi timbangan atau membiarkan konspirator berkumpul di kedai berakibat hukuman mati. Puncak kemanusiaan ada di Pasal 117: perbudakan utang dibatasi maksimal 3 tahun kerja, dan tahun ke-4 wajib dibebaskan tanpa syarat.

Pasal 100

"...he shall write down the interest on the money, as much as he has obtained, and he shall reckon its days, and he shall make returns to his merchant."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Agen niaga wajib mencatat bunga uang yang diperoleh dan menghitung harinya, kemudian menyetorkannya kepada saudagar pemodal.

Pasal 102

"If a merchant give money to an agent as a favour, and the latter meet with a reverse where he goes, he shall return the principal of the money to the merchant."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika modal diberikan sebagai pinjaman kebajikan tanpa bunga dan mengalami kerugian murni, agen cukup mengembalikan uang pokoknya saja.

103

Pasal 103 ★

Force Majeure Perjalanan
"If, when he goes on a journey, an enemy rob him of whatever he was carrying, the agent shall take an oath in the name of the god and go free."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika dalam perjalanan kafilah dagang seorang agen dirampok oleh musuh/gerombolan bandit, agen tersebut cukup bersumpah atas nama dewa bahwa perampokan itu di luar kuasanya, dan ia dibebaskan dari kewajiban ganti rugi kepada saudagar pemodal.

Pasal 104

Pasal 106

"If an agent obtain money from a merchant and have a dispute with the merchant (i.e., deny the fact)... the agent shall give to the merchant threefold the amount of money which he obtained."

Pasal 107

"If a merchant lend to an agent, and the agent return to the merchant... and if the merchant deny (receiving) what the agent has given to him... the merchant shall give to him sixfold the amount which he obtained."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Sebaliknya, jika saudagar kaya menyangkal telah menerima pengembalian modal dari agennya, saudagar itu didenda 6 KALI LIPAT!

108

Pasal 108 ★

Kecurangan Takaran
"If a wine-seller do not receive grain as the price of drink, but if she receive money by the great stone, or make the measure for drink smaller than the measure for corn, they shall call that wine-seller to account, and they shall throw her into the water."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika pemilik kedai minuman bir menolak pembayaran gandum dari rakyat jelata dan menuntut perak dengan batu timbangan berat yang dicurangi, atau mengecilkan takaran minum, wanita pengusaha itu diadili dan dilemparkan ke dalam air (ditenggelamkan).

Pasal 109

Jika para buronan/penjahat berkumpul di kedai minum dan pemilik kedai tidak melaporkannya ke istana, pemilik kedai dihukum mati.

Harga resmi minuman bir kredit: 60 ka bir dibayar 50 ka gandum saat musim panen tiba.

Pasal 112

"If a man be on a journey and he give silver, gold, stones, or portable property to a man with a commission for transportation... and that man take it to himself... that man shall deliver to the owner fivefold the amount."

Jasa ekspedisi kargo: orang yang menggelapkan titipan barang berharga wajib membayar ganti rugi 5 kali lipat.

Pasal 113

"If a man hold a (debt of) grain or money against a man, and if he take grain without the consent of the owner from the heap or the granary... he shall return as much grain as he took, and he shall forfeit all that he has lent..."

"If a man do not hold a (debt of) grain or money against a man, and if he seize him for debt, for each seizure he shall pay one-third mana of silver."

Pasal 115

"If a man hold a (debt of) grain or money against a man, and he seize him for debt, and the one seized die in the house of him who seized him, that case has no penalty."

Jika sandera utang meninggal secara wajar di rumah penyandera, tidak ada sanksi.

116

Pasal 116

"If the one seized die of abuse or neglect in the house of him who seized him... if it be a man's son (that he seized) they shall put his son to death; if it be a man's servant, he shall pay one-third mana of silver and forfeit whatever amount he had lent."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika sandera utang mati karena penganiayaan/kelaparan: jika anak debitur yang mati, anak kreditur dihukum mati! Jika budak, denda 1/3 mana perak dan utang hangus.

117

Pasal 117 ★

Batas Perbudakan Utang (3 Tahun)
"If a man be in debt and sell his wife, son, or daughter, or bind them over to service, for three years they shall work in the house of their purchaser or master; in the fourth year they shall be given their freedom."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seseorang terjerat utang dan menjual istrinya, anak laki-lakinya, atau anak perempuannya, atau menyerahkan mereka sebagai pekerja jaminan utang, mereka hanya boleh dipekerjakan selama tiga tahun di rumah pembeli atau tuannya; pada tahun keempat, mereka WAJIB DIBERIKAN KEMERDEKAAN MUTLAK.

Konteks: Reformasi sosial luar biasa dari Hammurabi untuk mencegah pemiskinan struktural warganya menjadi budak abadi (bandingkan dengan hukum Ibrani Keluaran 21:2 yang menetapkan 6 tahun).

cf. Kel. 21:2; Ul. 15:12 Pasal 118 ↓

Pasal 119

"If a man be in debt and he sell his maid-servant who has borne him children, the owner of the maid-servant (i.e., the man in debt) shall repay the money which the merchant paid (him), and he shall ransom his maid-servant."

Pasal 122

Pasal 123

"If a man give on deposit without witnesses or contracts, and at the place of deposit they dispute with him (i.e., deny the deposit), that case has no penalty."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika menitipkan tanpa saksi dan tanpa kontrak lalu pihak penerima menyangkal, pengadilan menolak gugatan tersebut (tidak ada sanksi).

Pasal 124

"If a man give to another silver, gold, or anything else on deposit in the presence of witnesses, and the latter dispute with him... they shall call that man to account, and he shall double whatever he has disputed and repay it."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika ada saksi sah namun penerima titipan berbohong menyangkalnya, ia wajib membayar dua kali lipat nilai barang titipan.

Pasal 125

"If a man give anything of his on deposit, and at the place of deposit either by burglary or pillage he suffer loss in common with the owner of the house... the owner of the house who has been negligent... shall make good (the loss)..."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika rumah tempat penitipan dibobol maling, pemilik rumah wajib mengganti kerugian barang titipan terlebih dahulu, lalu ia sendiri yang memburu pencurinya.

Pasal 126

"If a man have not lost anything, but say that he has lost something, or if he file a claim for loss when nothing has been lost... he shall double and pay for the (alleged) loss the amount for which he made claim."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Mengajukan klaim asuransi/kehilangan palsu berakibat hukuman membayar dua kali lipat nilai yang diklaimnya secara bohong.
