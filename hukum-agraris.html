Agraris, Irigasi Kanal & Perkebunan

Pasal 42 hingga 65 memuat hukum agraria Mesopotamia: sewa lahan bagi hasil, kelalaian pemeliharaan tanggul kanal air, moratorium bunga utang akibat bencana alam, dan budidaya kurma.

Logika Sosial: Hidrolika & Ekologi Mesopotamia

Dataran aluvial Babilonia sangat subur tetapi curah hujan sangat rendah. Kemakmuran kota bergantung 100% pada kanal irigasi buatan yang dialirkan dari Sungai Efrat. Karenanya, hukum memperlakukan kelalaian merawat tanggul irigasi sebagai ancaman eksistensial kolektif (Pasal 53–54). Di sisi lain, Pasal 48 memberikan perlindungan force majeure: bila badai Dewa Adad merendam ladang sehingga gagal panen, petani dibebaskan dari kewajiban menyetor gandum dan bunga pinjaman tahun tersebut dihapuskan.

42

Pasal 42

"If a man rent a field for cultivation and do not produce any grain in the field, they shall call him to account because he has not performed the work required in the field, and he shall give to the owner of the field grain on the basis of the adjacent (fields)."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Penyewa yang lalai mengolah ladang sehingga tidak menghasilkan gandum wajib membayar hasil panen kepada pemilik tanah berdasarkan rata-rata hasil ladang tetangga di sebelahnya.

Pasal 43

"If he do not cultivate the field and neglect it, he shall give to the owner of the field grain on the basis of the adjacent (fields); and the field which he has neglected he shall break up with hoes, he shall harrow, and he shall return it to the owner of the field."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Penyewa wajib membayar gandum dan harus mencangkul serta meratakan tanah sebelum mengembalikannya.

Pasal 44

"If a man rent an unreclaimed field for three years to develop it, and neglect it and do not develop the field, in the fourth year he shall break up the field with hoes... and shall measure out 10 GUR of grain per GAN."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Sewa lahan tidur 3 tahun untuk pembukaan lahan baru: jika ditelantarkan, di tahun ke-4 wajib digarap dan membayar denda 10 gur gandum per gan lahan.

Pasal 45

"If a man has given his field to a tenant for crop-rent, and receive the crop-rent of his field, and later Adad (i.e., the Storm God) inundate the field and carry away the produce, the loss (falls on) the tenant."

Jika sewa sudah diterima pemilik tanah lalu badai Adad membanjiri ladang, kerugian ditanggung penyewa.

Pasal 46

"If he have not received the rent of his field, and he have rented the field for either one-half or one-third (of the crop), the tenant and the owner of the field shall divide the grain which is in the field according to agreement."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika sistem sewa bagi hasil (setengah atau sepertiga panen), sisa panen dibagi sesuai kesepakatan.

Pasal 47

"If the tenant give the cultivation of the field into the charge of another... the owner of the field shall not interfere. He would cultivate it, and his field has been cultivated, and at the time of harvest he shall take grain according to his contracts."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Penyewa berhak mensub-kontrakkan penggarapan ladang kepada petani lain; pemilik tanah tidak boleh menghalangi selama menerima bagian sesuai kontrak.

48

Pasal 48 ★

Hapus Bunga Bencana
"If a man owe a debt, and Adad inundate his field and carry away the produce, or, through lack of water, grain have not grown in the field, in that year he shall not make any return of grain to the creditor, he shall alter his contract-tablet, and he shall not pay the interest for that year."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seseorang memiliki utang, lalu badai banjir Dewa Adad menyapu bersih tanamannya, atau karena kekeringan gandum tidak tumbuh, maka pada tahun bencana tersebut ia tidak perlu membayar gandum apa pun kepada krediturnya. Ia berhak membasahi dan mengubah prasasti kontrak utangnya, dan bunga pinjaman untuk tahun itu dihapuskan sama sekali.

Konteks: Klausul moratorium kredit bencana alam tertua di dunia, melindungi petani dari kebangkrutan massal.

Pasal 49

"If a man obtain money from a merchant, and give (as security) to the merchant a field... at the time of harvest the owner of the field shall receive the grain and sesame which is in the field, and he shall give to the merchant grain for the loan which he had obtained from him and for the interest..."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Pinjaman dengan agunan ladang: pemilik ladang yang memanen hasilnya, lalu menyetor pembayaran pokok utang beserta bunganya kepada saudagar.

Pasal 50

"If he give (as security) a field planted with (grain) or a field planted with sesame, the owner of the field shall receive the grain or the sesame which is in the field, and he shall return the loan and its interest to the merchant."

Pengembalian utang beserta bunga pada ladang berpanen wijen atau gandum.

Pasal 51

"If he have not the money to return, he shall give to the merchant (grain or) sesame, at their market value according to the scale fixed by the king, for the loan and its interest which he has obtained from the merchant."

Pasal 52

"If the tenant do not secure a crop of grain or sesame in his field, he shall not cancel his contract."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Penyewa yang gagal menghasilkan panen tidak boleh secara sepihak membatalkan kontrak sewa.

53

Pasal 53 ★

Tanggul Irigasi Jebol
"If a man neglect to strengthen his dyke and do not strengthen it, and a break be made in his dyke and the water carry away the farm-land, the man in whose dyke the break has been made shall restore the grain which he has caused to be lost."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana & Logika Sosial

Arti: Jika seseorang lalai memperkuat tanggul kanal air miliknya, sehingga tanggul tersebut jebol dan luapan airnya merusak ladang pertanian tetangga, maka orang yang tanggulnya jebol itu wajib mengganti seluruh gandum yang rusak.

Pasal 54

"If he be not able to restore the grain, they shall sell him and his goods, and the farmers whose grain the water has carried away shall share (the results of the sale)."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Jika ia tidak sanggup mengganti kerugian gandum itu, dirinya dan seluruh hartanya harus dijual, dan uang hasil penjualannya dibagi rata di antara para petani yang ladangnya kebanjiran.

Pasal 55

Pasal 64

"If a man give his orchard to a gardener to manage, the gardener shall give to the owner of the orchard two-thirds of the produce of the orchard... he himself shall take one-third."
Dalam Bahasa Sederhana

Sewa kebun kurma yang sudah berbuah: 2/3 hasil untuk pemilik kebun, 1/3 untuk tukang kebun pengelola.

Pasal 65

"If the gardener do not properly manage the orchard and he diminish the produce, the gardener shall measure out the produce of the orchard on the basis of the adjacent orchards."
